## A. seven windows (recommended)

**Device and form:** a moment in someone's day, one frame that rebuilds.

A flat apartment block at night, seven windows, one per coin. The lights go off as people go to bed, one window stays on, and the camera pushes into it to watch one real night of work.

- **First two seconds:** the block is fully lit, and a utc clock reads 23:59. Windows start going dark.
- **Beats:** the block goes dark → one window stays lit → push in: the clock runs from 00:19 and each payment drops a tick → 100 payments, $9.20 → last post 04:17 → 07:12: its fees come in, $47.31 → pull back: morning, and the window was never off → a dark window explains itself: under $1 of fuel, it sleeps → mark.
- **Look:** #0a0b0d night, flat hairline building, Cerulean light as the one accent, Geist and Geist Mono. No glow.
- **Sound:** a slow, sparse bed, a soft tick on each payment, and a light switch on each window.

## C. you slept

**Device and form:** life before and after, split screen to one.

Two panes share one clock: a phone on a nightstand that stays dark, and the agent's activity running beside it. In the morning the phone lights up, the panes close into one, and it shows everything that happened.

- **First two seconds:** a phone lock screen reads 23:58, then the screen turns off. The right pane starts running by itself.
- **Beats:** phone off → agent running, clock racing → payments stack up on the right while nothing moves on the left → 07:12: fees arrive → alarm: the phone wakes → panes merge into one summary: 100 payments, $9.20, $47.31 in → mark.
- **Look:** white ground on the agent's side, #0a0b0d on the phone's side, Base Blue on the summary, Geist.
- **Sound:** silence on the left and a pulse on the right, then an alarm tone that starts the music.
