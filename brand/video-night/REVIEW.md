# the night receipt: review

## Creative calls

- Built B of `STORYLINES.md`. Not built: seven windows, you slept.
- Second cut, after the first drew this note: 100 "thought" rows invite "all that thinking for one tweet?".
- Rows are now real posts, each with its topic and price. The left column carries the agent's own words in large type, at the time it wrote them.
- The blue goes black from the slot during the music's quiet stretch, for the 03:11 line. It is the one accent flood in the film.
- The loop closes on the paper: fees in, then fuel added.
- The header says "paid to: bootr's ai relay". The seller is Bootr, and the film says so.
- Not claimed: that nobody acted that night, or that the agent is autonomous.

## Measured

- `check` passes. Render: 1920x1080, 29.7 s, -17.2 LUFS, peak -1.5 dBFS. That is about 1 dB under the -16 target with the bed already at full level, so it plays a little quieter than the other films.
- Quotes are asserted verbatim against the saved posts at build time. Posts + other thinking are asserted equal to the total.
- Looked at: QA frames from the check run, the final render at the 03:11 quote.

## Not checked

- What the $4.50 of "other thinking" did. It needs the supervisor's records, and that read was refused for production data.
- Whether what the agent's posts say about third parties is correct. The film uses only lines with no names in them.

## Weakest shot
