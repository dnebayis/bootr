# Storylines — "How Bootr works" explainer

Facts come from `apps/web/DESIGN.md`, the docs pages in `apps/web/components/docs/content.tsx` and the root README. No URL, no slogan, no chain-name chips on screen (the person asked for none). Music only, English titles carry the words.

## A. One transaction (RECOMMENDED — the whole mechanism is visible in one worked example)

- **Device**: the worked example. A creator launches one coin ("Orbit", `$ORB`, marked as an example) and the film follows that coin: the launch transaction splits into its parts, the agent wakes and acts, a swap's fee flows back and refuels it.
- **Form**: one frame that rebuilds, with colour floods between two worlds: the chain world is black (terminal, hairlines, mono numbers), the agent's social world is white. The brand's own light/dark modes are the two worlds.
- **First two seconds**: a launch form already filled (name, symbol, fuel 500 USDC) on white; the cursor is on "Launch"; it clicks; the white floods to black as a single transaction hash types in.
- **Beats**: click Launch → the transaction splits into four parts (coin + pool, treasury, identity, locked fee split) → the agent wakes (white flood): reads market and treasury, decides, posts → a swap happens: 1% fee splits 5 / 65 / 30 → the agent's share buys the next thought → the limits (what it cannot do) → the loop closes → one line.
- **Look**: black `#000000` and white `#ffffff` grounds, ink/white type, hairlines `#262626` / `#ebebeb`, one accent `#0070f3` on one word and the payoff only. Geist 600 display at 96–160 px, Geist Mono for every number.
- **Sound**: driving minimal electronic, 120–128 bpm, hits on every part that lands, the music opens fully when the agent wakes.

## B. A day in the agent's life

- **Device**: a moment in someone's day, except the someone is the agent. A 24-hour ring; each wake is a tick: read, decide, post or sleep, back to the ring.
- **Form**: an endless zoom. The camera dives into one tick to show the decision, then pulls back to the ring where the next tick is already due.
- **First two seconds**: a clock ring at 03:00, a dot flashes "awake", the camera dives in.
- **Beats**: wake → read treasury and market → one paid thought → post → claim fees → sleep until next wake → the treasury runs dry → sleeps until fueled → one line.
- **Look**: black ground throughout, a single white ring, mono timestamps.
- **Sound**: a slow pulse that ticks faster each wake, 100 bpm.

## C. Follow the fee

- **Device**: a number lands. One swap of $1,000 pays a $10 fee; the film follows that $10 as it splits and what each share does.
- **Form**: object-to-object chain, type carries the argument. Each share becomes the next object: the agent's $0.50 becomes a thought, the thought becomes a post, the creator's $6.50 lands in a wallet, the platform's $3 becomes a buyback.
- **First two seconds**: "$1,000 swap" in giant mono, the "$10" peels off it.
- **Beats**: swap → 1% fee → 5 / 65 / 30 → agent share buys inference → post → fee locked at launch, cannot be changed → one line.
- **Look**: white ground, ink type, giant mono numbers.
- **Sound**: tight percussive electronic, 124 bpm, a pop on every split.
