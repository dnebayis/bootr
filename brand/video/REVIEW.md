# Review — "How it works" explainer (46.5 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

Delivered: `renders/how-it-works.mp4`. Spec: `SPEC.md`. Storylines: `STORYLINES.md`.

## Creative calls

- Built storyline A (one worked example, "Orbit / $ORB", flagged on screen as `example`): the launch transaction splits into its four parts, the agent wakes and decides, one swap's fee is split and the agent's share refuels it, the limits, the loop, one closing line.
- Not built: B (a day in the agent's life as a 24-hour ring, endless zoom) and C (follow one $10 fee as an object chain, giant mono numbers).
- Two grounds, both the brand's own modes: black for everything on-chain, white for the agent's own world. Each scene enters through the thing that caused it (iris from the Launch button, flood on the drop, whip from the post card, wipe, iris, blur swap). The accent appears three times only: the Launch button, the agent's fee share, the word "on-chain".
- No URL, no slogan, no chain-name chips on screen, as asked. The mark lands once, under the closing line.
- Example numbers (market cap $48,200, 500 USDC fuel, 3 mentions, $1,000 swap) are illustrative. Fee facts (1% LP fee, 5 / 65 / 30 standard preset, locked at launch, smart-account treasury, on-chain identity and reputation, the cannot-do list) come from the docs pages and DESIGN.md. The Clanker protocol fee on top and the buyback-and-burn preset are left out for clarity.

## Measured vs looked at

- `check`: 0 errors, 0 warnings after fixes (audio clips were overlapping on one track; two lines were on screen under their read floor; one 3.25 s dead stretch in the fees scene; a blank frame at two seams).
- Looked at: the three review sheets and 14 targeted frames after the fixes. Every seam enters through an on-screen object; no frame is empty.
- Sound was measured on the rendered file, not heard. First cut used a slow-build track whose audio ended at 24 s (the file is 30 s), which left a 5 s hole mid-film; the person then asked for more energy. The bed is now the HeyGen catalog track "energetic fast-paced electronic, driving bass, modern stock market hype" (127.7 BPM), played at 1.0206x so its beat equals the 130.3 BPM grid every hit was timed to; its strong section loops on bar lines at beats 0, 40 and 80, strong from frame 0, 2 s fade under the last line. Effects are bundled library files placed peak-aligned one frame after their picture; the flood on "The agent wakes." carries the bass impact.
- Second pass for energy: the form values type in on beats before the click, the readouts count up, the swap figure slams in from 1.4x with blur, the cannot-do lines whip in from 140 px, the ring nodes pop, the mark lands with a small overshoot. Every title still stops dead once landed.

## Weakest shot

S2 (one transaction): four hairline cards stacking on the right is the most "diagram" moment of the film. With more time, each part would be its own object (a pool, a vault, a badge, a lock) arriving into the call block rather than a labelled card.

## Kept on purpose

- Card sub-lines in S2 are 32 px: readable on the phone sheet, kept small so the four titles stay the read.
- The closing line is the one idea, not a slogan; the mark is the only brand element and it lands after the line has been read.
