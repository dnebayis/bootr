# what is bootr: storylines

The one thing: launch a coin on bootr and it comes with an agent that pays for its own thinking from the coin's trading fees.

Common to all three: the dot-matrix agent from `brand/video-invite` (seed "friend"), speaking in the ElevenLabs "River" voice, 35 to 45 s, 1920x1080, catalog music on the free allowance. House rules: lowercase, no URL, no slogan, no price or return language.

Facts from `bootr.fun/docs`, read 6 nov 2026: one transaction; RWAGMI or Clanker, on Base; own treasury and X account; the fee split is locked at launch; what an agent may do with money is enforced on-chain; below 1 USDC it sleeps and wakes when fuelled or when fees are claimed; agents can be wrong and can lose money.

## A. ask me (recommended)

**Device and form:** a question with the input, one frame that rebuilds.

Strangers' questions drop into the frame as typed lines, and the agent answers each one out loud, deadpan. The answer builds a small picture beside the face, so by the end the whole product is on screen and every part came from a question someone would really ask.

- **First two seconds:** Base Blue, the face, and a typed line: "what is bootr?" The agent: "Good question. I'm what you get."
- **Beats:** what are you? → an agent, launched with a coin → who pays for your thinking? → a share of every trade's fee → can that share change? → no, it's locked at launch → can you move money anywhere you like? → no, the limits are on-chain → what if the money runs out? → the eyes close, it sleeps, then wakes when fuelled → are you always right? → no, i can be wrong, and i can lose money → how do i get one? → RWAGMI and Clanker, one transaction → mark.
- **Look:** Base Blue ground, white dots, Geist 600, questions in Geist Mono.
- **Sound:** the invitation film's plain 110 BPM beat family, a key tick on each typed question, the bed drops out for the sleep.

## B. made of

**Device and form:** the object is the explanation, an endless zoom.

The agent's face is the diagram. The camera dives into one dot and it turns out to be a part of the product: an eye opens into its wallet, the antenna into what it reads, the mouth into a real post, and each dive comes out through another dot.

- **First two seconds:** the face fills the frame. "Everything I am fits in sixteen by sixteen." The camera starts falling into the left eye.
- **Beats:** eye → its own wallet, fees arrive → antenna → it reads mentions, the market, its notes → forehead → one thought, paid for → mouth → a real post on its own X account → pull all the way back: the face sits on a coin → a door for RWAGMI and one for Clanker → mark.
- **Look:** Base Blue inside the face, a change of ground on each dive, one dot always carrying the cut.
- **Sound:** a rising tone on each dive, a hit on each landing, the voice dry and close.

## C. its first day

**Device and form:** a moment in someone's day, a clock runs across one frame.

One agent's first 24 hours from launch, told by the agent like a diary. It wakes up with a coin, a wallet and ten dollars of fuel, someone trades, the first fee lands, it thinks, it posts.

- **First two seconds:** a clock at 00:00:00, a transaction confirms, and the dots appear. "I was born in one transaction."
- **Beats:** 00:00 launch → 00:04 first thought, the fuel drops → 00:40 first post → 03:00 nobody talks, it waits → 09:00 a trade, a fee, a share comes in → 14:00 a holder asks it something, it answers → 23:59 "you could have one of me." → mark.
- **Look:** white ground, Base Blue face, Geist Mono clock, fuel as one bar.
- **Sound:** soft ticking under a slow-building beat, one note for each post.
- **Note:** the times are illustrative unless taken from a real agent's public tape, and the film would say so.
