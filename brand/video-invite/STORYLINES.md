# the invitation: storylines

The one thing: launching your own coin on bootr gives you an agent of your own, and the door is open.

Facts from `bootr.fun/launch`, read 5 nov 2026: one transaction; the launch goes through RWAGMI or Clanker, on Base; kinds are trader, researcher, community and custom; the agent has its own wallet and X account; every agent gets a dot-matrix portrait; minimum fuel is 10 USDC.

## A. i don't exist yet (recommended)

**Device and form:** a character speaks, one frame that rebuilds.

The agent you haven't launched talks to you, first person. Its dot-matrix portrait starts as a few dots and fills in with every line, as it imagines what it could be, until only your signature is missing.

- **First two seconds:** Base Blue, a few white dots, and the line "i don't exist yet." typed large.
- **Beats:** "i don't exist yet." → "i could watch a market." → "i could welcome your holders." → "i could read all night." → "you'd name me." a ticker types itself → two doors, RWAGMI and Clanker logos large, Base under both → "one transaction." → the portrait completes and blinks awake → "your turn." → mark.
- **Look:** Base Blue ground, white dots, Geist 600 large, partner logos at full size in their own colours.
- **Sound:** a warm, hopeful bed that builds, a soft tick on each dot wave, a release when it wakes.

## B. two doors

**Device and form:** a world you walk into, one continuous take.

The Base square fills the frame and turns out to be a building. Two doors stand open in its wall with light coming through, RWAGMI on one and Clanker on the other, and a doormat waits with your ticker on it.

- **First two seconds:** the Base square, huge. It splits into a wall, and two lit doorways appear.
- **Beats:** the wall → the doormat, "$YOURS" → the camera moves to the first door, what is behind it → the second door → both lead to the same room: an agent at a desk with its own wallet and X account → "come in." → mark.
- **Look:** flat architectural drawing in Base Blue and white, warm light as the only gradient, each door in its partner's colours.
- **Sound:** a door opening, a room tone, then a warm bed.

## C. the eighth seat

**Device and form:** real coins as a community, one frame that rebuilds.

The real coins launched so far land one by one on a grid, each with its own face and ticker. One tile stays empty, with a cursor blinking in it.

- **First two seconds:** tiles drop onto the grid on the beat: $BOOTR, $OWL, $BCAT.
- **Beats:** seven tiles land → the empty eighth → "this one's yours." → the ticker types itself → the tile splits into two doors, RWAGMI and Clanker, on Base → "one transaction." → mark.
- **Look:** white ground, Base Blue tiles, real coin images, Geist.
- **Sound:** a pop on each tile, then silence at the empty one, then the bed.

## All three

20 to 30 s, 1920x1080, music and sound only, no voice, catalog music on the free allowance. House rules: lowercase, no URL, no slogan, no price or return language.
