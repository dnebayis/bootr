# Go / no-go for the $BOOTR launch: production read-only verification, 2026-11-04

Read-only pass over the whole chain on 2026-11-03 22:10-22:25 UTC: Base mainnet (public RPC
`base-rpc.publicnode.com`), the Hetzner box (`docker exec`, `docker logs`, `psql` reads), the public endpoints,
the built-in browser (no wallet connected), and the local test suites. Nothing was sent, spent, restarted or
changed on the server. Secrets are reported as lengths only. Deployed image commit: `22f6681` (all five
services); local `HEAD` `d84998c` differs from it only in `brand/` (no code diff in `apps/`, `packages/`, the
compose file).

**Verdict: GO on the software, NO-GO on funding until the treasury wallet is topped up** (section 4). Every
service, contract read, relay leg, indexer and page checked out; the one hard blocker is that the wallet the
plan says launches $BOOTR (`PLATFORM_TREASURY` `0x55e6…9d82`) holds 0 USDC and 0.003 ETH, which cannot pay the
20 USDC fuel or a dev buy.

## What only a real mainnet launch can prove

1. The launch transaction itself on v3 (`BootrLauncher.launch` → RWAGMI B20 deploy, pool, LP lock, guardian
   registration, ERC-8004 `agentId`, `LaunchContext`), the B20 salt/address derivation and the fee-split slots in
   `RwagmiLpLockerV3.launchInfo` (the fork tests cover this path on a fork; mainnet gas, the real factory state and
   the opening price impression at `STARTING_TICK -243400` are only seen live).
2. The supervisor's onboarding on mainnet: `registerAgent` from the poll, the admin's `approveSessionKey` tx, the
   self-bundled enable-mode `handleOps` (kernel prefund from the deposit or the admin's top-up), `validationConfig`
   read-back, Builder Code registration.
3. The paid relay leg: a real EIP-3009 authorization from the agent's spender, CDP facilitator `verify`/`settle`
   on Base, OpenRouter completion, the `x-bootr-receipt`, and the unknown-outcome reconciliation.
4. The treasury loop with real fees: `settleAndClaim`/`claimQuote` to the kernel, `BootrSwapHelper` WETH→USDC at
   the Chainlink bound, `fund_model` to the spender under the windowed cap, `fund_gas` unwrap.
5. The X path end to end: `@bootrfun` connect with `media.write`, draft-id rebind after the launch, first post
   and reply, media upload.
6. The dev buy and the first trades through the 1 % hook fee, and the indexer picking the new token up on the v3
   launcher (`tokens.totalCount` → 2, `/market/<BOOT>` populated, `BOOT_TOKEN_ADDRESS` set afterwards).

## Pre-launch checklist (from the findings)

1. Fund the treasury `0x55e6…9d82`: ≥ 20 USDC fuel + the dev-buy ETH + ~0.002 ETH for fee and gas. (Blocker.)
2. Optionally top the admin `0x2C97…D9b3` up to ~0.02 ETH so onboarding survives a gas spike.
3. Connect `@bootrfun` from the launch form before launching (gets `media.write`), or accept a silent coin.
4. Use an allow-listed model id in the launch (`anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6`, not `claude-sonnet-4-5`).
5. After the launch: `BOOT_TOKEN_ADDRESS` on the indexer, `bootToken` in the admin console, `launchInfo` and
   `agentInfo` read-backs per `mainnet.md` §6 step 5.
6. Housekeeping, not blocking: fix or drop `apps/web/e2e/agent-posts.spec.ts`; extend the compose `-u` lists.
